## Release evidence to attach before legal sign-off

1. TimeWall publisher confirmation of placement, exact Goblins Wood campaign/offer ID, Level 130 event ID and server-side completion timestamp. Confirm the provider permits an additional external promotion, and whether old installs qualify.
2. The actual participant eligibility countries, age rule, provider time limits and disqualification/chargeback policy. The owner must choose whether existing users can opt in and how the start date treats prior milestones.
3. Reconciliation record matching publisher completion ID to Earn identity, provider approval, reversal status, approved revenue and settlement period. The current postback hash binds user ID and revenue only; free-text offer name, detail and transaction ID cannot establish a winner alone.
4. Bank/system-wallet evidence for **real available funding** independent of pending TimeWall revenue, staking principal and referral liabilities. Approved EUR/THC valuation source, timestamp, conversion precision and locked THC quantity for all three awards. Record reserve transfer/reference and prevent double commitment.
5. Technical acceptance: one opt-in per person, duplicate detection, provisional ranking, ties decided by publisher-confirmed completion timestamp then stable provider event ID, pending/approved/reversed status, seven-day Earn hold, appeal route, immutable award and reversal journal. No auto-credit before approval.
6. Correct organizer identity, contact/complaints channel, eligibility geography, prize transfer method, identity verification and retention period, approved copy and final video/banner. Do not claim guaranteed publisher revenue per player.

- Is this skill/progress promotion treated as a contest, sweepstake, gaming promotion or another category in each eligible country? Does the specific game/offer or token prize trigger additional requirements?
- What precise terms, publication/filing steps, exclusions, age and residency checks, dispute process and winner notification/acceptance period apply? Can existing installations participate if they opt in after rules publication?

## Separate Earn/staking release boundary

The monthly Earn leaderboard is a different competition; its `prizes_enabled` remains false. Walletless THC principal staking is available with a 0.09% stake/unstake policy for new positions; the displayed 8%/12% THC and 5%/8% THR APY values are seeded projections, not a funded dynamic Pythia quote or payable rewards. New THR stake is currently rejected without a signed wallet intent. Flex / six-month / one-year terms, funded APY cap and floor, lock/exit rules, reserve reconciliation and biometric transaction approval remain a separate engineering release. Contest funding may never be taken from staking principal or estimated future BTC/TimeWall proceeds.
