Thronos Earn — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to Thronos Earn, a beta rewards interface operated by ThronosLabs within the Thronos ecosystem. For privacy requests, contact admin@thronoschain.org.

2. Data we process

To operate an Earn Account, we process an email address, a password hash rather than a plaintext password, session-token hashes, account identifiers, and reward-ledger records. If a user elects to link a Thronos wallet, we process the public wallet address, public key, and signed challenge metadata required to verify the link. We do not request or store private keys or seed phrases.

3. Provider data

For CPX Research, Thronos Earn generates a stable opaque provider identifier server-side. We do not send the Earn email address or optional wallet address to CPX through the offerwall identity. Provider receipts may contain a transaction reference, reward amount, offer reference, provider status and reversal information needed for fraud prevention and accounting.

4. Why we use data

We use this data to authenticate users, display account activity, verify provider callbacks, prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards, handle reversals, respond to support requests, and protect the service. We do not sell Earn Account personal data.

5. Third-party services

When you open a provider offerwall, that provider operates its own service and privacy practices. Review the provider’s policy before submitting information in an offer or survey.

6. Retention and requests

We retain operational and security records only for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, fraud prevention, provider reconciliation, legal obligations, and dispute handling. You may request access, correction, or deletion information by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

7. Security

We use server-side session controls, password hashing, signed wallet-link challenges, provider callback verification, and idempotent ledger records. No security measure is absolute; never send secrets or wallet recovery material by email or chat.

Read the Terms of Use or visit Contact.
