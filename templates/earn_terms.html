Thronos Earn — Terms of Use

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Scope

Thronos Earn is a beta rewards interface operated by ThronosLabs within the Thronos ecosystem. It lets an authenticated Earn Account access third-party offer providers, where available. A Thronos wallet is optional and is not required to create or use an Earn Account.

2. Third-party offers

Offers, qualification, survey availability, completion decisions, reversals and provider payment status are determined by the relevant provider. Thronos Earn does not guarantee that a user will qualify for, complete, or receive a reward from any particular offer.

3. Reward records

A reward is recorded only after a verified server-to-server provider receipt. A pending record is not spendable, payable, transferable, redeemable, or a promise of payment. Provider clearance and reversals may change the state of the original record. No browser action or local timer can clear a reward.

4. Account and security

You are responsible for safeguarding your Earn Account credentials. Optional wallet linking requires an expiring signed challenge and wallet approval. Never share a private key, seed phrase, password, session cookie, or recovery material with anyone claiming to represent Thronos Earn.

5. Fair use

Users must not use automation, false identities, manipulated devices, duplicate accounts, replayed requests, or any method intended to interfere with provider qualification or reward accounting. Suspected abuse may result in account restriction and provider review.

6. Beta status

Features may change while the service is in beta. At present, this page does not enable cash-out, BTC payout, wallet unlock with THC, staking, lending, pool participation, or any financial return.

7. Contact

For support, privacy, provider, or account questions, contact admin@thronoschain.org. See also the Privacy Policy and Contact page.
